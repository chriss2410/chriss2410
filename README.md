📖 The core principles I look for in great teams/projects:

1. Garbage in, Garbage out - AI will not solve your data quality issues. First fix your data, then you can work on your use cases to boost effeciency with AI.
2. Strive for AI complete products, build feedback & automated improvement mechanisms. Remove human bottlenecks as much as possible, let GPUs do the hard work.
3. A players want to work with other A players, focus on building a team where the skill & ambition levels are aligned.
4. Ideas are cheap, execution is hard. Don't worry about sharing your ideas, learn details fast, focus on execution.
